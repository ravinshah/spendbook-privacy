Privacy Policy

SpendBook · Effective 25 July 2026 · Version 1.1

The short version: SpendBook runs entirely on your iPhone. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Your financial data never leaves your device.

1. Overview

SpendBook is a personal finance app that runs entirely on your device. We do not operate any servers, do not collect analytics, and never transmit your data anywhere. Everything stays on your iPhone.

2. Data We Collect & Why

We store only data you explicitly enter or import:

  • Expense amount & currency — to calculate totals and reports
  • Vendor & sub-category — to identify and categorise expenses
  • Date — to filter and trend your spending over time
  • Category & notes — to organise and search your records
  • Receipt images (optional) — for reference and on-device OCR extraction
  • Bank and credit card statements (optional) — PDF files you choose to import are read on your device to extract transactions

Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6): Legitimate interest — you are the sole data controller of your own device.

3. How Your Data Is Protected

  • Database: SwiftData store with NSFileProtectionCompleteUnlessOpen — encrypted when the device is locked.
  • Receipt images: Encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage. The encryption key is held in the iOS Keychain and never leaves your device.
  • Statement passwords: If you choose to let SpendBook remember a PDF password, it is stored only in the iOS Keychain on this device and is never synced or transmitted.
  • App access: Protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode on every open.
  • Export files: Written with file protection and removed after sharing.

4. Statement Files & Receipt Images

Statement PDFs and receipt images may contain personally identifiable information such as your name, address, partial card numbers, or contact details. All of it is processed entirely on your device — parsing, OCR, and analysis never involve a network request. Imported PDFs are read once to extract transactions and are not retained by the app.

Receipt images are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being stored, are never transmitted, and can be deleted individually from an expense or in bulk via Settings → Clear All Data.

5. Artificial Intelligence

SpendBook uses no cloud AI services. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, some features (expense chat, receipt understanding, the monthly Money Story) are enhanced by Apple's on-device language model. On all other iPhones the same features run on built-in rules. In both cases processing happens on your device and nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

6. Third-Party Frameworks

SpendBook uses only Apple system frameworks — none of which transmit your data:

  • Vision — on-device OCR for receipts and image-based statements
  • PDFKit — reading statement PDFs locally
  • Swift Charts — local chart rendering
  • LocalAuthentication — Face ID / Touch ID
  • SwiftData — local encrypted storage
  • CryptoKit — AES-256 encryption
  • FoundationModels — Apple's on-device AI, where supported

The only optional network request the app can make is to fetch public currency exchange rates. No personal or financial data is included in that request.

7. Your Rights (GDPR Art. 15–20)

  • Right of Access — view all your data within the app at any time.
  • Right to Rectification — edit any transaction directly in the app.
  • Right to Erasure — Settings → Clear All Data permanently deletes all records immediately.
  • Right to Data Portability — export everything as Excel, CSV, PDF, or JSON at any time.
  • Right to Restriction — stop using the app and delete all data at any time, without contacting anyone.

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and immediate control.

8. Data Retention

Data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. You can set an automatic retention period in Settings → Data Retention to auto-delete records older than a chosen number of years.

9. Data Breach

Since no data is transmitted to or stored on external servers, there is no risk of a server-side data breach. Your data can only be accessed through your device with your biometric or passcode.

10. Children's Privacy

SpendBook is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, you will be shown an updated consent screen on next launch and asked to agree again.
